Welcome to Our Family

Rooted in Christ, Serving Coventry Since 2001

Cornerstone Church of God was established in 2001 in Coventry as a spiritual haven for Tamil-speaking families, students, and professionals making the UK their home.

Under the pastoral leadership of Pastor Clint Pratheepan, our congregation gathers weekly on Howard Street to worship in truth, study the Scriptures, and build loving relationships that span across generations.

On 16 March 2025, God blessed us with our first branch — Rugby Tamil Cornerstone Church of God — extending our spiritual ministry to Warwickshire.

Convenient Arrival

Directions & Parking in Coventry

Clear parking guidance for Sunday visitors attending our main sanctuary on Howard Street, Coventry.

Free On-Street

Jenner St & Arthur St

Streets adjacent to Howard Street offer on-street parking options within a 2-3 minute walk. Check council signs for Sunday restrictions.

Pay & Display

Bird Street (CV1 5FX)

Bird Street public pay-and-display car park is situated within easy walking distance and offers ample spaces for Sunday visitors.

Convenient

Stoney Stanton Road

Public spaces along Stoney Stanton Road beside St Marks Coventry Church, only a short 3-5 minute stroll from Howard Street.
