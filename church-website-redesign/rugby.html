Our Story & Calling

About Cornerstone Church of God, Rugby

Cornerstone Church of God, Rugby, was established on 16 March 2025 as the first branch of our parent church, Cornerstone Church of God, Coventry, which was established in 2001.

We give thanks to God for His faithfulness and for the privilege of serving the people of Rugby through the love and Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Foundation

The Meaning of Cornerstone

In the Bible, a cornerstone is a foundation stone that provides strength, stability, and alignment to a building. In the same way, Jesus Christ is our Chief Cornerstone, the foundation of our faith, our church, and our ministry. Everything we believe, teach, and do is rooted in Him and guided by His Word.

• Ephesians 2:20 • Psalm 118:22
Our Vision

A Christ-Centred Church

Our vision is to build a Christ-centred church where people can worship God, grow in His Word, experience the power of prayer, and share the Good News of salvation through Jesus Christ. Through worship, prayer, fellowship, and outreach, we seek to see lives transformed, families strengthened, and believers united in faith and love.

• Matthew 16:18 • Zechariah 4:6
Our Desire & Calling

The Great Commission

Our desire is to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teach the truth of God’s Word, and help people grow in their relationship with the Lord. As the first branch of Cornerstone Church of God, Coventry, we seek to serve the community of Rugby faithfully, fulfil God’s purpose for our church, and bring glory to His name.

• Matthew 28:19 • 1 Corinthians 3:11
“I will build My church, and the gates of Hades shall not prevail against it.” • “Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit, says the Lord of hosts.” — Matthew 16:18 • Zechariah 4:6 —

Church Calendar

Weekly & Monthly Programmes

We warmly invite you to join us. Whether you are seeking God, longing for prayer, or looking for a church family, you are welcome at Cornerstone Church of God, Rugby. May we continue to grow together in faith, love, and service to our Lord Jesus Christ.

Every Sunday 4:00 PM

Sunday – Tamil Worship Service

Join us for a time of worship, praise, prayer, and the teaching of God’s Word in Tamil. We gather as a church family to glorify the Lord Jesus Christ, grow in faith, and encourage one another in His love.

📍 15 Oliver Street, Rugby CV21 2ET • 4:00 pm – 6:00 pm
Every Tuesday 8:30 PM

Tuesday – Night Prayer (via Zoom)

Join us online for a dedicated time of prayer for our church. Together, we seek God’s guidance, pray for the growth of His work, and intercede for our church family and its needs.

💻 Online via Zoom • 8:30 pm – 9:30 pm
Every Thursday 6:30 PM

Thursday – Rugby City Prayer Walk

We walk and pray throughout the streets and areas of Rugby, bringing the town, its residents, families, and communities before the Lord. We pray for God’s protection, peace, and blessing over the city, and against the evil schemes in the city.

🚶 Rugby Town Centre & Communities • 6:30 pm – 7:30 pm
3rd Saturday 11:00 AM

Every Third Saturday – Fasting Prayer & Street Evangelism

Set apart this time to seek the Lord through worship, His Word, prayer, and fasting. We also seek opportunities to share the love and good news of Jesus Christ with the people of Rugby, trusting God to work in hearts and lives.

✞ 15 Oliver Street & Town Evangelism • 11:00 am – 1:00 pm
3rd Sunday 4:00 PM

Every Third Sunday – Holy Communion Service

Remembering the sacrifice of our Lord Jesus Christ on Calvary and sharing in Holy Communion together as a unified body of believers.

🍷 15 Oliver Street Sanctuary • 4:00 pm – 6:00 pm
