Youth Ministry

Inspiring a Generation Rooted in Christ & Unashamed in Faith

"Let no one despise you for your youth, but set the believers an example in speech, in conduct, in love, in faith, in purity." — 1 Timothy 4:12

The Youth Ministry is an integral, dynamic part of our church, committed to nurturing and equipping young people to thrive in their daily Christian walk.

Bi-Weekly Gatherings: Thoughtfully planned meetings address modern youth challenges, practical biblical apologetics, student mentoring, and professional journeys.

Active Service: Youths take leadership across the church — leading contemporary praise, operating sound projection, and participating in tract evangelism.

Sunday School Ministry

Nurturing Young Hearts in the Love & Knowledge of God

"Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it." — Proverbs 22:6

Our Children's Ministry affirms that children are a precious gift from God and an integral part of His Kingdom, taught through creative, age-appropriate approaches.

Compassionate Teachers: Led faithfully alongside dedicated teachers who cultivate foundational Christian values: kindness, respect, obedience, prayerfulness, and love.

Annual Highlights: Regular classes feature Scripture memory verse competitions with awards, creative quizzes, drama presentations, and the annual Sunday School Camp.

Worship & Media Team

Cultivating an Atmosphere of Holy Adoration & Digital Reach

"Ascribe to the LORD the glory due his name; worship the LORD in the splendor of holiness." — Psalm 29:2

Our Worship & Media Team leads the congregation into meaningful worship and ensures the Word of God reaches more people through technology and digital platforms.

Anointed Music & Praise: Our Worship Team faithfully leads the congregation into meaningful encounters with God through passionate Tamil hymns and English praise.

Excellence in Media: Media engineers ensure crisp audio mixing, multilingual visual projection, and high-definition global livestreaming to families around the globe.

Community Outreach Ministry

Extending Christ's Love Beyond the Confines of the Building

"You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden." — Matthew 5:14

The Church Outreach Ministry serves as a vital expression of our mission to extend God's love to the local community and wider society through practical care.

Active Service: Activities include regular food hamper distributions for struggling families, hospital visits, assistance for the homeless, and street tract evangelism.

BBC Radio Recognition: Honored with an invitation from BBC Coventry & Warwickshire Radio (CWR) to sing Christmas carols live on air, sharing our heritage with the public.

Global Missions

Sharing God's Love, Empowering Communities & Planting Churches

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." — Matthew 28:19

Our Global Missions department responds to the Great Commission by bringing Christ's transformational message to vulnerable communities across the world.

Church Planting: Providing direct financial assistance, spiritual backing, and pastoral mentorship to native church planters establishing new ministries.

Emergency Relief: Mobilizing swift humanitarian relief during crises, including food aid, clean water initiatives, and rebuilding sanctuaries overseas.

Family & Sacraments Ministry

Celebrating Milestones of Faith: Dedication, Baptism & Prayer

"As for me and my house, we will serve the LORD." — Joshua 24:15

The Family & Sacraments Ministry walks alongside households through life's most sacred milestones, fostering Christ-centred homes and generational faith.

Covenant Celebrations: Conducting infant and child dedications, believer's water baptisms, home cottage prayer gatherings, and pastoral family counseling.

Sacramental Care: Guiding believers into meaningful water baptism by full immersion and celebrating Holy Communion each month in reverence and thanksgiving.

Church Committee, Deacons & Ministry Helpers Team

Honoring Dedication, Unity & Humble Service Behind the Scenes

"But all things should be done decently and in order." — 1 Corinthians 14:40

Cornerstone's Deacons, Officers & Committee serve humbly behind the scenes, ensuring the smooth spiritual, logistical, and financial stewardship of God's house.

Godly Governance: Upholding high standards of integrity, property maintenance, statutory charity compliance, and ministry coordination under pastoral guidance.

Servant Heart: Working in unity to support weekly services, pastoral visitation, hospitality meals, and sound governance for the growing congregation.

Men's Fellowship

Equipping Men as Spiritual Pillars in Family & Society

"Be watchful, stand firm in the faith, act like men, be strong. Let all that you do be done in love." — 1 Corinthians 16:13–14

The Men's Fellowship provides a valuable forum for men within the church to deepen their faith, establish strong spiritual foundations, and encourage one another in their daily Christian walk.

Mission & Calling: Dedicated to equipping men to sustain their discipleship journey, enabling them and their families to bear faithful witness to Christ in contemporary society.

Key Activities: Monthly gatherings feature dedicated prayer, Bible study, and candid discussions on faith, family life, workplace challenges, and annual recognition awards.
Activities ? Join Fellowship ?

Women's Fellowship

A Welcoming Sisterhood of Prayer, Encouragement & Spiritual Formation

"She opens her mouth with wisdom, and the teaching of kindness is on her tongue." — Proverbs 31:26

The Women's Fellowship is a vital part of our church community, offering a welcoming, prayer-centred environment where women grow together in faith and Christian fellowship.

Sisterhood & Formation: Meeting every second week of the month, gatherings include focused intercessory prayer, structured Bible study, honest discussion, and spiritual reflection.

Retreat & Outreach: Members engage in charitable outreach supporting vulnerable families in Coventry, highlighted by the uplifting Annual Women's Spiritual Retreat.

Family Fellowship

Building Relationships Through Faith and Community

"And let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together." — Hebrews 10:24-25

Our Family Fellowship brings believers together in warm, Christ-centered gatherings where families share meals, study Scripture, and support one another in faith. These intimate home-based meetings create space for authentic relationships and spiritual growth.

Through regular prayer meetings, Bible study sessions, testimonies, and intercession groups, members experience the power of Christian community. Families of all ages gather to worship, encourage one another, and grow together in discipleship.

Fellowship Activities: Home gatherings • Prayer meetings • Scripture sharing • Testimonies • Intercession • Community meals • Spiritual encouragement
