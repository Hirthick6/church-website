Sunday – Tamil Worship Service

Every Sunday | 4:00 pm – 6:00 pm

Join us for a time of worship, praise, prayer and the teaching of God’s Word in Tamil. We gather as a church family to glorify the Lord Jesus Christ, grow in faith and encourage one another in His love.

Tuesday – Night Prayer via Zoom

Every Tuesday | 8:30 pm – 9:30 pm

Join us online for a dedicated time of prayer for our church. Together, we seek God’s guidance, pray for the growth of His work and intercede for our church family and its needs.

Thursday – Rugby City Prayer Walk

Every Thursday | 6:30 pm – 7:30 pm

We walk and pray throughout the streets and areas of Rugby, bringing the town, its residents, families and communities before the Lord. We pray for God’s protection, peace and blessing over the city and for His will to be fulfilled in Rugby.


Every Third Saturday – Fasting Prayer and Street Evangelism

Every third Saturday of the month | 11:00 am – 1:00 pm

Set apart this time to seek the Lord through worship, His Word, prayer and fasting. We also seek opportunities to share the love and good news of Jesus Christ with the people of Rugby, trusting God to work in hearts and lives.


We warmly invite you to join us. Whether you are seeking God, longing for prayer or looking for a church family, you are welcome at Cornerstone Church of God, Rugby. May we continue to grow together in faith, love and service to our Lord Jesus Christ.
