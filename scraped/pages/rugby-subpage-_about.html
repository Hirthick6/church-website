ABOUT US

About Cornerstone Church of God, Rugby

Cornerstone Church of God, Rugby, was established on 16 March 2025 as the first branch of our parent church, Cornerstone Church of God, Coventry, which was established in 2001.

The Meaning of Cornerstone

In the Bible, a cornerstone is a foundation stone that provides strength, stability and alignment to a building. In the same way, Jesus Christ is our Chief Cornerstone, the foundation of our faith, our church and our ministry. Everything we believe, teach and do is rooted in Him and guided by His Word.

We give thanks to God for His faithfulness and for the privilege of serving the people of Rugby through the love and Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.

OUR VISION AND MISSION

Our vision is to build a Christ-centred church where people can worship God, grow in His Word, experience the power of prayer and share the Good News of salvation through Jesus Christ. Through worship, prayer, fellowship and outreach, we seek to see lives transformed, families strengthened and believers united in faith and love.

Our vision is rooted in the words of our Lord Jesus Christ: “I will build My church” (Matthew 16:18). We trust in His promise and stand firm in the truth that God’s work is accomplished “Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit” (Zechariah 4:6)

OUR COMMITMENT

Our desire is to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teach the truth of God’s Word and help people grow in their relationship with the Lord. As the first branch of Cornerstone Church of God, Coventry, we seek to serve the community of Rugby faithfully, fulfil God’s purpose for our church and bring glory to His name.

Jesus Christ is our foundation, the Word of God is our guide, and the Great Commission is our calling. All glory belongs to God for what He has done, what He is doing and what He will continue to do through His church.
