LEADERSHIP

The leadership of Cornerstone Church of God, Rugby, has been entrusted to Bro Sanjeev by Pastor Clint Pradeepan of Cornerstone Church of God, Coventry.

Bro Sanjeev serves with humility, faithfulness and a sincere commitment to the calling God has placed upon his life, seeking to lead the church in accordance with God’s Word and to serve the congregation with love and dedication.

Under his leadership, the church seeks to grow in faith, unity and spiritual maturity, remaining rooted in prayer, guided by the Holy Spirit and faithful to the teachings of Scripture, all to the glory and honour of Jesus Christ.
